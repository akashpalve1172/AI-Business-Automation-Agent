TECHNOVA SOLUTIONS
BUSINESS TRAVEL POLICY

Policy Version: 1.0
Effective Date: January 1, 2026

1. Business Travel

Employees traveling for approved business purposes may
claim eligible travel expenses.

2. Transportation

Economy-class air travel is permitted for domestic business
trips.

Train travel may be booked according to the employee's
eligible travel class.

3. Hotel

Employees may claim hotel expenses up to INR 4,000 per night
for domestic business travel.

4. Meals

Employees may claim reasonable meal expenses up to
INR 800 per day during approved business travel.

5. Receipts

Employees must submit valid receipts and supporting documents
with their expense claim.

6. Approval

Business travel must be approved by the employee's manager
before booking.

7. Reimbursement

Approved expenses are normally processed through the company's
expense management system.
