TECHNOVA SOLUTIONS
WORK FROM HOME POLICY

Policy Version: 1.0
Effective Date: January 1, 2026

1. Purpose

This policy defines the rules and guidelines for employees
working remotely.

2. Work From Home Eligibility

Full-time employees who have completed their probation period
are eligible to work from home, subject to manager approval.

3. Work From Home Allowance

Eligible employees may work from home for a maximum of
2 days per week.

Employees should coordinate their work-from-home schedule
with their reporting manager.

4. Restrictions

Employees may be required to work from the office for
important meetings, training sessions, team activities,
or business requirements.

5. Equipment

Employees working remotely are responsible for maintaining
company-provided laptops and other equipment.

6. Support

For technical problems while working remotely, employees
should contact the IT Support team.

7. Policy Updates

The company may modify this policy based on business needs.
Employees should refer to the latest policy version for
current requirements.
