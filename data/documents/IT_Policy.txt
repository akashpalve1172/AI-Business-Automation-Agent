TECHNOVA SOLUTIONS
IT SECURITY POLICY

Policy Version: 1.0
Effective Date: January 1, 2026

1. Password Security

Employees must use strong passwords for company systems.

Passwords must not be shared with other employees.

2. Account Security

Employees must not share their company account credentials.

Employees should lock their computer when leaving their
workstation unattended.

3. Security Incidents

Employees must immediately report suspected security
incidents to the IT Security team.

4. Lost Equipment

Lost or stolen company devices must be reported to the
IT Support team as soon as possible.

5. Software Installation

Employees must not install unauthorized software on
company-owned devices.

6. Remote Access

Employees accessing company systems remotely must use
company-approved secure access methods.

7. Support

For technical support, employees should contact the
IT Support team.
