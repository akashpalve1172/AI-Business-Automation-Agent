
TECHNOVA SOLUTIONS
EMPLOYEE EXPENSE POLICY

Policy Version: 1.0
Effective Date: January 1, 2026

1. Purpose

This policy defines the rules for submitting and
reimbursing eligible employee business expenses.

2. Eligible Expenses

Employees may claim reasonable business expenses
incurred while performing approved company activities.

Eligible expenses include approved travel, meals,
local transportation, and business-related purchases.

3. Expense Submission

Employees must submit expense claims through the
company expense management system.

Expense claims should normally be submitted within
15 days of the expense date.

4. Receipts

Employees must provide valid receipts or supporting
documents for reimbursable expenses.

5. Approval

Expense claims require approval from the employee's
reporting manager.

6. Non-Reimbursable Expenses

Personal purchases and expenses unrelated to approved
business activities are not reimbursable.

7. Questions

Employees with questions regarding expense claims
should contact the Finance team.
