TECHNOVA SOLUTIONS
LEAVE POLICY

Policy Version: 1.0
Effective Date: January 1, 2026

1. Annual Leave

Full-time employees receive 24 days of annual paid leave
per calendar year.

2. Sick Leave

Employees receive 12 days of sick leave per calendar year.

3. Leave Approval

Employees should submit leave requests through the
company leave management system.

Leave requests should normally be submitted at least
3 working days before the planned leave.

4. Emergency Leave

In an emergency, employees should notify their reporting
manager as soon as possible.

5. Unused Leave

Unused annual leave may be carried forward according to
company rules.

6. Support

Employees with questions about leave should contact HR.
